Tournament Information

Our annual Warrior Classic is back! Get your team ready for a challenging and exciting summer tournament! This premier summer tournament is hosted in the west end of Ottawa, at Walter Baker Park, Ben Franklin Park & Wesley Clover Fields. The tournament is open to all Regional, iModel, DC1, L3, L4 or LDP (Quebec clubs) teams ages U12-U18. OPDL and PLSJQ teams will not be allowed to participate. Each division will follow a round-robin format followed by finals for the group winners!
