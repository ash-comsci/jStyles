The Details

A Crest Worth Singing About

Our class hoodie celebrates everyone who makes music at All Saints. It's perfect for concert days, field trips and showing your ASH pride all year long.

  • 🎨 Colour: Classic red with a chrome-silver, gold and black front print
  • 🧵 Style: Pullover hoodie with lined hood, drawstrings and ribbed cuffs and waistband
  • 👕 Fit: Unisex, relaxed. If you're between sizes, size up for a roomier fit
  • 🧺 Care: Machine wash cold, inside out. Tumble dry low and do not iron the print

Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

The hoodie is designed for All Saints Harmonics students, but families and friends are welcome to order too. Just add extra hoodies when you check out on School Cash Online.

We ask that all orders go through School Cash Online so sizes and payments are tracked together. If you can't use School Cash Online, contact the All Saints Music Department to make other arrangements.

Open the youth or adult sizing chart above, or use the Quick Size Finder. The best way to check is to measure a hoodie that already fits well, laid flat, and compare it to the chart.

Hoodies are custom-printed in one order, so exchanges may not be possible once they arrive. Please check the sizing carefully before you check out.
